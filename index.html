New to Rhyme Scene?

Rhyme Scene uses your existing Puter account. New accounts must be created directly on Puter.

If you tap “Sign in” without an account, Puter creates a temporary account with no email. This causes verification issues later.

To avoid this, create your account first.

If you already clicked “Sign in” by mistake, clear Puter cookies and start again.
